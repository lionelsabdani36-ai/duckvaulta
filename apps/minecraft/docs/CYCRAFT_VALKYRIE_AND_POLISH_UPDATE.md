* **Structural Specifications:**

### 2. Kinetic Cyber Jump Pads (Skybridge Transit)
* **Mechanics:** Engineered in `CycraftCyberQuest.jar` v1.5.0 (`onPlayerInteract` vector physics).
* **Launch Trigger:** Stepping on heavy weighted pressure plates with sea lanterns beneath.
* **Departure Launchers:**
  * North Exit (0, 65, -11): Boosts players North toward CySec Lab.
  * South Exit (0, 65, 11): Boosts players South toward Monorail & Stargate.
  * East Exit (11, 65, 0): Boosts players East toward Gladiator Colosseum.
  * West Exit (-11, 65, 0): Boosts players West toward Bastion Fortress.
* **Return Launchers:** Matched booster pads positioned at all 4 sector terminals for high-speed return transit.
* **Sensory Feedback:** Sound effects (`FIREWORK_ROCKET_LAUNCH` + `BEACON_ACTIVATE`) and custom on-screen title banner.
